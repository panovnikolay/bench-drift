Detector: an 8×8 sliding window over the medians of the last 22 reports; the change point is located by the standardised two-sample statistic, significance by the Mann–Whitney test. The effect threshold rises to 1.5·CV of the channel, so noisy benchmarks are not flagged as regressions.
